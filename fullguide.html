Unit 1: Electricity

Circuit Components & Their Symbols

Understand the definitions and relationships between voltage, current, and resistance.

Formula: V = IR, where V is voltage, I is current, and R is resistance.

Series vs Parallel Circuits

Identify how components are arranged and how they affect voltage and current.

Unit 3: Nervous & Endocrine Systems; Body Defense

Nervous System

  • CNS (Brain & Spinal Cord) and PNS (nerves throughout the body).
  • Neurons: cell body, dendrites, axon, myelin sheath.
  • Reflex Arc: Involuntary response pathway.

Endocrine System

  • Main glands: Hypothalamus, pituitary, thyroid, adrenal, pancreas, gonads.
  • Key hormones: Insulin, adrenaline, thyroxine, etc.

Body Defense

  • Immune System: White blood cells, antibodies.
  • Three lines of defense: Physical barriers, innate immunity, adaptive immunity.
